## The source text (read this)

Leo Perutz died on 25 August 1957.

- **USA:** published 1918 (before 1929) → **public domain**. Project Gutenberg
  ([#36901](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36901)) hosts the text on this basis.
- **EU (incl. Germany/Austria):** protected for **70 years from the end of the
  year of death** (§§ 64, 69 UrhG) → in copyright **through 31 December 2027**,
  **public domain from 1 January 2028**.

Until then this repository:

- ships **no full text** of the novel;
- uses **short quotations only**, each as an evidentiary excerpt attached to a
  mapped place and its commentary, under the **right of quotation (§ 51 UrhG)**;
- links to the Gutenberg text for context; whether that text is accessible
  depends on the reader's jurisdiction.

The geodata itself (coordinates, routes, clock times, editorial glosses) is an
original factual compilation — facts are not copyrightable — and is licensed
CC BY-NC 4.0. Coordinates and routes derive from OpenStreetMap
(© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL); basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors
© CARTO.

*From 1 January 2028 the EU restriction lapses; the full-text/NER layer can
then be added like in the sibling Joyce project.*
